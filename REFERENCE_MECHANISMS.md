## Cross-reference synthesis

**Preserve:** an iconic centerpiece (meaningful not decorative); clean, monumental typography; professionally art-directed materiality; varied slide architecture; intelligible technical/financial graphics; disciplined visual pacing and contrast; precision in chart provenance; proof before spectacle.

**Reject:** art direction based only on orbit/ring/halo; repeated centered slogans; unlimited gradients; tiny unreadable metric panels; re-skinned Canva templates; fake growth numbers; identical 3-card layouts on every slide; over-technical fetishization; copied brand assets; inaccessible microtext.

**Success feel:** private, composed, intelligent, intriguing, slightly uncanny, never preachy: "I feel invited to recognize or challenge this interpretation, not measured and filed." For an investor: "I can understand the engine, imagine the premium experience, and identify the next falsifiable commercial hypothesis."
