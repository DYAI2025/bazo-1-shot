## Product definition — PROJECT INTENT (not proof of launch)

**Brand name:** Bazodiac = BaZi + Zodiac.

**One-line thesis:** A living atlas of self-interpretation, combining two distinct astrological traditions through a traceable computational fusion model and offering people the last word on meaning.

**Canonical brand sentence:** "The system offers a language. The individual owns the meaning."

**Audience hypothesis, not demonstrated market segmentation:** internationally minded, design-literate, financially comfortable, high-agency professionals and self-development enthusiasts. They are curious about spiritual traditions, but want agency, nuance, provenance and craft rather than superstition, fatalism, manipulative affirmation or clinical diagnosis. A narrative about self-reflection is not a clinical-effect claim.

**Seven distinct source perspectives:** Western Sun, Moon, Ascendant; BaZi Year Pillar, Month Pillar, Day Master, Hour Pillar. The FuFirE Fusion Field is a relationship/comparison layer, *not* an eighth card. The word "Signature" is reserved for a possible later concept; avoid naming the current field Signature.

**FuFirE = Fused Firmament Engine.** Concept: Western calculations and Chinese BaZi/Four Pillars are independently computed; selected, explicitly versioned features from each are projected with documented model rules into a five-dimensional Wu Xing space: Wood (木), Fire (火), Earth (土), Metal (金), Water (水). Outputs can compare normalized distributions, calibrated similarity/difference and component contribution provenance. Mapping Western attributes to Wu Xing is a chosen interpretive model, not a proven historical equivalence or psychological law. The 5D framework enables computational reproducibility and critique, not scientific confirmation of astrological personality insights. Do not display synthetic or raw harmony as a truth-score.

**Semantic reading grammar:** Source/claim provenance -> offered reading -> LIGHT (resource/strength) and SHADOW (contextual excess or cost) -> one honest, concrete question -> user response (resonates / partly / not for me), with freedom to revise, decline, or remove. No judgment, no fatalistic language, no point predictions, no diagnosis, no therapy replacement. Light and Shadow refer to possible expressions of a *symbol*, never to good/bad kinds of people. The product's authority covers the methods it describes and its editorial respect, not the user's ultimate identity.

**Daily experience concept:** choose a perspective for the day from one's personal atlas, see a reflective interpretation and gradually discover contextual facets. The product aims to support recurring voluntary reflection; no retention outcome has been established. Avoid streak guilt, compulsory self-improvement, performance scoring, and fixed identity labels.

**Premium hypothesis:** opt-in Levi/Eve voice conversation with chart-based context and user-approved conversation summaries across sessions; personal depth, revision and conversation continuity, not agentic pronouncements. Historic Astro-Noctum donor has portions of a profile-summary persistence flow; New_Bazi has an ElevenLabs widget; full integrated new-consumer production continuity is NOT VERIFIED. Do not claim that user memory/data deletion or permissions are fully implemented.

**Later / related product:** Bazodiac Relationship (Chance & Shadow): evidence-bound two-person interpretation, agency and mutual consent, no soulmate or destiny score; prototype, not verified production feature.
